What ShiftKind is

ShiftKind is a job board. Adult family homes in Washington State post shifts, and caregivers apply for them. ShiftKind is not an employer, a staffing agency or a care provider. We do not hire caregivers, set their pay, supervise their work or pay them. The home and the caregiver make their own arrangement, and the home pays the caregiver directly.

  • Applying for a shift does not guarantee you will get it. If you accept a shift, turn up, or tell the home as early as you can.

Checks we make

We look at uploaded credentials and home licences and mark the ones we have reviewed. A review is not a guarantee. Homes should make their own checks before a caregiver starts, and caregivers should satisfy themselves about a home before accepting a shift.

Packages and payment for homes

A new home has a 14-day free trial. After that, posting shifts needs a paid package at the price shown in the app. Packages are billed monthly by card through Stripe and renew each month until you cancel. You can cancel at any time from the My home tab; your package then runs to the end of the month you have paid for. We do not refund part months unless the law requires it. Sales tax is added where it applies.
