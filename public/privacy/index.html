ShiftKind

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

ShiftKind is a job board for Washington State. It lets caregivers find shifts and lets adult family homes post them. This page says what we collect when you use it, why, and who can see it.

What we collect

From everyone with an account: your name, email address, phone number, ZIP code, sex if you choose to give it, and whether you signed up as a caregiver or a home.

A picture, if you add one: a caregiver's photo or a home's photo or logo. We shrink it before saving, which also removes hidden details such as where the photo was taken. You can change or remove it at any time.

From caregivers:

  • the credentials you upload (the document, its number and its expiry date)
  • the days and nights you say you are free
  • the area you are willing to work in, as a point on the map and a distance
  • a short note on the kind of work you want, if you write one
  • the shifts you apply for, your messages with homes, and the ratings homes give you

From homes:

  • the home's name, address, phone number, licence number and position on the map
  • how many residents you care for, by level of need
  • the shifts you post, your messages with caregivers, and the ratings caregivers give you

Payments: if a home pays for a package by card, the card is entered on Stripe's page, not ours. We never see or store the card number. We keep a record of which package you have and when it renews.

Notifications: if you turn on notifications on a phone or computer, we keep the address your browser gives us for that device, so we can tell you when a message arrives. Turning notifications off removes it. We also record which of your messages have been read, and when we last emailed you about a conversation.

Technical records: the companies that host ShiftKind keep ordinary server logs, such as the time of a request and the address it came from.

How we use it

  • to show caregivers the shifts that fit them, and to show homes the caregivers who are free
  • to check that a credential or a home licence is real
  • to let a caregiver and a home message each other
  • to send account emails, such as the link that confirms your address
  • to tell you by email, and by a notification on your phone if you turned that on, that someone has sent you a message. These say who wrote to you. They never contain the message itself.
  • to answer you when you ask for help

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see what

  • Anyone signed in can see a home's name, address, phone number, licence number, position on the map and open shifts.
  • Signed-in homes can see a caregiver's first name and surname initial, free time, working area, note on the work they want, ratings and credentials, including the uploaded documents.
  • Profile pictures can be seen by anyone signed in to ShiftKind. They are not open to the public web.
  • Messages are seen by the caregiver and the home in that conversation.
  • The person who runs ShiftKind can see accounts, credentials, homes and shifts in order to check them and to give support.

Companies that help us run ShiftKind

  • Supabase stores the accounts, records and uploaded documents, in the United States.
  • Cloudflare delivers the website and forwards email sent to our addresses.
  • Stripe takes card payments from homes.
  • Resend sends our account emails and new-message emails.
  • Apple, Google or Mozilla, whichever makes your browser, delivers phone notifications to your device.
  • OpenStreetMap supplies the map. When you search for an address, the words you type are sent to its address search.

When we check a nursing assistant's credential number, we look it up in Washington State's public record of health care providers.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself: open the Me tab and choose Delete my account. This removes your profile, your uploaded documents, your shifts or applications, your messages and your ratings, and it stops any card subscription. It cannot be undone. Copies held in our database provider's backups are overwritten on its own schedule.

You can also ask us to correct or delete your information by emailing support@getshiftkind.com.

Age

ShiftKind is for adults. You must be 18 or older to have an account.

Security

Your information travels over an encrypted connection, and each account can reach only what the lists above describe. No system is perfectly safe, so please use a password you do not use anywhere else.

Changes to this page

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
